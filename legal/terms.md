## The service

Nexa is operated by Jona Klassen, [ADDRESS], Ireland ("the operator"). Nexa helps student groups plan group assignments. Members upload an assignment brief, split tasks on a shared board, record completed work in a contribution log and export a contribution statement.

These terms apply between you and the operator when you use Nexa. By creating an account you accept these terms and confirm that you have read the privacy policy.

## Plans and prices

Nexa has three options. The current prices are shown on the Pricing page and at checkout before you pay. [TO REVIEW: whether VAT applies and how prices are shown.]

- Free plan: each account can create 1 project with 1 brief breakdown and 1 contribution statement draft.
- Project unlock, 2 euro, paid once: either 1 more project with 3 brief breakdowns and 3 contribution statement drafts, or 3 more of each on an existing project.
- Monthly plan, 7.99 euro a month: unlimited projects, brief breakdowns and statement drafts, within the fair use limit below.

A project is paid for by the member who creates it. Members who join a project use that project's allowance and pay nothing. Drafting a contribution statement again counts as a new draft. A failed attempt does not count. The current usage of a project is shown in the app.

Fair use: to protect the service from automated or abusive use, a project on the monthly plan can use up to 30 brief breakdowns and 30 statement drafts. The operator can also stop AI features for all users for the rest of a day when a daily limit for the whole service is reached.

## Payment, renewal and cancellation

Payments are processed by Stripe. The monthly plan renews automatically each month on the date you first paid, until you cancel it. You can cancel at any time in Account settings under Manage billing; the plan then stays active until the end of the month you have paid for, and is not charged again.

When a monthly plan ends, your projects and their data stay. AI features on projects created under the plan stop until you subscribe again or unlock the project for 2 euro. Unused project unlocks do not expire while your account exists. They are not refunded when you delete your account. [TO REVIEW: refunds on account deletion.]

## AI output

Nexa uses an AI model to extract details from briefs, propose tasks and estimated hours, and draft contribution statements. AI output can be incomplete or wrong. It is a suggestion that your group reviews and edits before it is used. Check extracted details against the original brief and check statements against the contribution log before you rely on them.
