## Creating a group and project

Sign in with your email address or with Google. On Your projects, choose New group and project. Enter a group name, the module code, the assignment title and the final deadline as DD/MM/YYYY, then choose Create and continue. You become the owner of the group.

A group has at most 8 members. Each project belongs to one group.

## Uploading the brief

On the Brief page, upload the assignment brief as a PDF of up to 10 MB, or paste the text into Or paste the brief text. Nexa sends the brief to an AI model, which lists the deliverables, deadlines, word counts and marking criteria. This usually takes under 30 seconds. You can leave the page while it runs; the result is saved.

A failed attempt does not count against your allowance. If the brief cannot be read, try pasting the text instead.

## Reviewing the extracted details

Check the extracted brief against the original. You can edit, add or remove deliverables, deadlines, word counts and marking criteria. Dates use DD/MM/YYYY and times use 24 hour HH:MM. Nothing is created until you choose Save and propose tasks.

## The task proposal

Nexa proposes tasks with estimated hours based on the word counts in the brief, and an even split of hours between members. You can change task titles, owners and hours, remove tasks or add new ones. The panel on the right shows each member's share of the hours. Suggest a new split shares the hours as evenly as the tasks allow.

If the brief's final submission date differs from the project's final deadline, Nexa shows both dates and lets you choose which one the project uses. Tasks due after the final deadline are pointed out. Choose Create tasks to add them to the board.

## The task board

The task board has three columns: To do, In progress and Done. Open a task to change its status, owner, due date or hours, and to attach links to your work in Google Drive, OneDrive or SharePoint. Use the member buttons above the board to see one member's tasks. Changes appear for every member within a few seconds.

When a member marks a task as done, the other members can confirm the work or flag it with a reason. You cannot confirm your own task. A task due after the project's final deadline is marked After final deadline.

## The contribution log

The contribution log records every task created, status change, file link, confirmation and flag, with the member's name, the date and the time. Entries are added automatically and cannot be edited or deleted by any member, including the group owner. The summary shows tasks completed, estimated hours, confirmations and flags for each member.

## Exporting the contribution statement

On Contribution statement, choose Draft statement. Nexa drafts a factual statement for each member from the contribution log. Member names and the log are sent to the AI model; email addresses and file links are not. The draft is marked as AI output. Check every statement against the log, use Edit text to correct it, then choose Export PDF or Copy text.

The statement shows the dates of the recorded activity it covers. If the log changes after the draft, the page says how many changes the draft does not include, so you can draft it again or edit the text.

## Inviting members

Only the group owner can invite members. On Invite members, copy the invite link or show the QR code for teammates to scan. A link works for 7 days and for a limited number of joins, and the owner can revoke it at any time. You can also send an invite by email; that invite only works for the email address it was sent to.

A person who opens an invite sees the group name, the project, the first names of members and the extracted brief, then signs in to join.

## Calendar feed

On Calendar feed, create a private link and add it to Google Calendar, Apple Calendar or Outlook as a subscription. The feed lists your tasks and the project deadlines. Calendar apps refresh subscriptions every few hours, so changes can take time to appear. Anyone with the link can read the feed, so do not share it. Regenerate the link if it has been shared by mistake; the old link stops working at once.

## Installing the app

Nexa works in any modern browser and can be installed like an app. On an iPhone, open Nexa in Safari, choose the Share button, then Add to Home Screen. On Android, open Nexa in Chrome, open the menu and choose Install app. On a computer, use the install button in the address bar of Chrome or Edge. The installed app only stores the application files, not your data.

## Plans and billing

The free plan includes 1 project with 1 brief breakdown and 1 contribution statement. A project is paid for by the member who creates it; members who join a project do not use their own allowance.

You can unlock one more project for 2 euro, paid once. A paid project includes 3 brief breakdowns and 3 contribution statements. You can use the unlock for a new project, or to add allowance to the current project when it runs out.

The monthly plan costs 7.99 euro a month and includes unlimited projects, brief breakdowns and statements, within a fair use limit. You can cancel at any time in Account settings under Manage billing. Projects and their data stay when a subscription ends; AI features on those projects stop until you subscribe again or unlock the project for 2 euro.

Payments are handled by Stripe. Nexa never sees or stores your card details. See Pricing for the current prices.

## Deleting your data

In Account settings, Download my data gives you a JSON file with your profile, groups, tasks, file links, log entries and statements. Delete account deletes your account at once. Groups where you are the only member are deleted with their files. In other groups, your log entries stay so the record stays complete, and they show Former member instead of your name.

A group owner can delete the group and everything in it in Group settings. Projects are deleted automatically 30 days after the last activity or the final deadline, whichever is later. Every member is emailed 7 days before, and any member can keep the project.

## Contact

For questions about your data, email the address in the privacy policy. Nexa replies within one month.
